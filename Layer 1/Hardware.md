## UTP Cable Transmission Pairs
- Non switch devices
	- 1-2 = transmit pair
	- 3-6 = receive pair
- Switches
	- 1-2 = receive pair
	- 3-6 = transmit pair
- emit faint signals outside of the cable which can be copied
- vulnerable to electromagnetic interference

## Cables 
- Straight Through Cable
	- used for non switch device to switch
- Crossover Cable
	- Used for nonswitch to non switch connectoins
	- Used for switch to switch connections

## Fiber Connections
- requires a small form-factor pluggable (SFP) transceiver
- The wire itself is made from the ff:
	- 1. The fiber glass core
	- 2. A cladding that reflects light
	- 3. A protective buffer
	- 4. The outer jacket of the cable
- has two modes - single mode and multimode

| type            | core diameter                 | multiple angles?                                             | cable length                                | price           |
| --------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| singlemode (sm) | narrower than multimode fiber | Light enter in a single angle from a laser based transmitter | allows longer cables than utp and multimode | expensive       |
| multimode (mm)  | wider than single mode fiber  | allows multiple angles to enter fiber glass core             | longer than utp<br>shorter than sm          | cheaper than sm |
